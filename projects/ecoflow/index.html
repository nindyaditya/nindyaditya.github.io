Ecoflow

EcoFlow is a travel app concept designed to make Bali tourism more seamless, fair, and sustainable. Powered by real-time telco data, the app helps travelers avoid overcrowded spots, supports local businesses, and rewards eco-friendly choices.

CHAPTER 1 : THE PROBLEM

A trip to Bali always makes us excited - but not what comes with it

Stuck in traffic for hours.

Arriving at a destination only to find it overcrowded.

That moment when excitement turns into exhaustion.

“A trip to Bali always makes us excited, but not the idea of being stuck in traffic for hours, only to find out the destination is overcrowded.”

Rizaldy - Traveller

Three problems, one messy situation

The Traveler's Dilemma

You want authentic experiences but end up in overcrowded hotspots. Hours wasted in traffic and crowds.

The Community Gap

Popular areas thrive while hidden gems struggle. Tourism money flows to the same places.

The Invisible Impact

Nobody is measuring the environmental cost. Traffic and waste add up, but no one sees the full picture.

OUR MISSION

We envisioned a seamless tourism experience in Bali - one that rewards exploration, distributes benefits fairly, and protects the environment.

CHAPTER 2 : UNDERSTANDING REAL PEOPLE

CHAPTER 3 : FROM INSIGHT TO ACTION

When good ideas meet reality - the messy, beautiful design process

We prioritized features that solved core problems over nice-to-haves, then mapped every decision point, moment of friction, and question a traveler would ask.

Home Dashboard

Personalized recommendations surface nearby destinations based on crowd levels and preferences.

Smart Explore

Category-based discovery with instant filtering by interest type.

Crowd Intelligence

A visual heatmap displays five-day crowd predictions by hour.

Adaptive Itinerary

Timeline planning integrates activities with real-time crowd data.
